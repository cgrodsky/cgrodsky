Link to Windows 12

Connect to your PC

Open Phone Link on your Windows 12 PC and scan its QR code, or type the 5-character code it shows.

End-to-end encrypted. Your phone connects directly to the PC (WebRTC). PeerJS's free broker only introduces the two devices; it never sees your photos or messages. Nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere else.

Check the number

Make sure your PC shows the same number, then select Yes, they match on the PC.

Waiting for the PC…

Phone Link messages — text between this page and your PC. These aren't SMS.

Send photos

Pick photos and they're saved to Pictures on your PC.

Send a notification

It pops up on your PC and appears in Phone Link › Notifications. (A web page can't read your other apps' notifications.)
