Phone Link

Connect to your PC

Open Phone Link on your Windows 12 PC and scan its QR code, or type the 5-character code it shows.

End-to-end encrypted. Your phone connects directly to the PC (WebRTC). PeerJS's free broker only introduces the two devices; it never sees your photos, messages or screen. Nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere else. Once paired, your phone and PC also see each other's battery and basic device details (in Devices), only while they're connected.
